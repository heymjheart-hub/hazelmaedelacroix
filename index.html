Who Is Hazel Mae?

Hazel Mae Delacroix is a sweetheart to some and menace to others. Depends on which parts you get to see. She's new in town. Born in Georgia, but grew up in the north. Detroit to be exact. Her greatest influence is her daddy and grandmomma who often spoke slow and in stories. You might find her do the same. Up north, she went to school so she knows a lot aobut the mind of people and can actually have full-blown conversations with horses. 3 Years ago, she was in a horse accident, and now she's working through her trauma, yet still not ready to take on driving a horse, though she's okay to ride.

Her daddy was a gunslinger. He sent her up north for schooling and protection. Now he's gone. He left behind a journal and a big secret and she's back in the area to find out what that secret really is. In the journal is a list of the following names. Hazel needs to find these men to uncover that secret.

The Journal

Entry One · May 12 · 1896
Georgia smells the same as I remember, wet soil and gun powder. My grandmomma always said Georgia remembers you even when you forget it. I believe her now more than I did as a lil girl.

I arrived today but haven't met a soul. That's fine. I'm patient. I've always been patient.

I just know daddy was tryin to tell me something in that journal he left me. So in case I lose my life on this escapade, I'm gone leave something behind. I don't quite know what to expect here, but I'll watch. I'll smile. I'll remember everything.

and I'll be ever so patient.
— H.M.D.
Entry Two · May 17 · 1896
This morning I woke up at the Saint Hotel in Valentine. I didn't find nothin there except dirt and grime. I did meet two men today. I would call them gentlemen but I don't know them well enough yet. Mr.Earl & Mr. Thomas. Mr.Thomas kindly chauffered me around and we met another man in St.Denis. He was a kind man who had an unusual amount of lottery tickets. I think I might have offended him. His name was Mr.Oswald. If I did, he quickly forgave me and helped me with some target practice right outside of Blackwater. I told him about the horse accident 3 years back. He seemed understanding but he was very inquisitive about the was the horses responded to me... very inquisitive.

So far, no one knows why I'm really here. I'm actually hoping my neww acquintences don't know anything about my daddy and this secret. I don't want them to be involved. They seemed nice enough.
— H.M.D.
Entry Three · May 23 · 1896
I've been thinkin on the last week and I still haven't recovered from it. From having to kill 6 men, one of which was a sacrifice for a very strange ritual brought on by Mr. Klause Deveaux. After that I'm still thinking about it. Klause said he spoke to one of the Spirits to find Justice O'Dell (a name on my daddy's list) To be quite honest, I think the curse of Bellefontaine never went away, it just moved to Blackwater, as we've seen some strange things happen there. So to BlackWater I go!

Thomas Shaw is the person I know the most... and as things are getting more and more dangerous, I've decided to tell him about the journals and wehere to find them. In the event that I am killed, Thomas Shaw is to take this journal and destroy it. Thomas, if you're reading this, stop being nosey, and destroy this right now!
— H.M.D.
Entry Four · May 26 · 1896
Well. The leaf tea is piping. I've discovered that a man named Josiah Blackwater was responsible for the murder of many communities years ago. He had 14 offspring and left them to head to St. Denis. The Curse of Bellefontaine originated with Josiah, and has continued. Josiah also had run ins with the Red Jackets. I do believe my newest acquintances are part of that group, which I believe they now call the Crimson Cloud. Mr. Klause seems to be their go-to. I've trusted him until this point but there's something that's not quite sitting right about him. Particularly that comment he made about my daddy and the spirit realm. We will see.

In other news, I've met Hostess. She's the large horse that belongs to Goliath. She's the first horse that I've ridden comfortably since the accident. Goliath said she'd be happy to have me again, so I may take her up on that. It's difficult to navigate these parts with Honey (my current horse and carriage).
— H.M.D.

Session History

What Has Happened

Episode 1 Highlights
Hazel explored Valentine, St. Denis, and Blackwater. Has met Earl Benzo, Thomas Shaw, and Oswald Marlow. Hazel revealed her fear of horses due to a horse accident 3 years ago. Shared the Curse of Bellefontaine.
Episode 2 Highlights
Hazel Mae met Klause Devoe. She and her travel companions met in St. Denis to vanquish the Curse of Bellefontaine. SOMETHING changed, but She does not believe the curse is done. Hazel found a list of names in her daddy's journal. She believes it's a hit list and she needs to kill the men on it for her daddy. She shared this with Thomas Shaw and requested his assistance. She asked him to destroy the journals in the case of her death. Hazel has killed 7 people - 1 she hasnt told when and how, 5 robbers attempting an ambush, 1 sacrifice.
Episode 3 Highlights
Hazel met several new people. Learned that Josiah Blackwater was responsible for the Curse of Bellefontaine. (see journal day 4). Hazel rode a horse named Hostess and is slowly getting over her trauma.
Episode 4 Highlights
Hazel had a run in we 2 strangers in the county. She informed Klause and Tatsu of Josiah story. She joined the ranks of the Crimson Cloud group and won a knife fight with Klause. Help the medics completed a buttering procedure with pommade."
Episode 5 Highlights
Hazel met Tate Love and PC who both are now tied closely to her story. Hazel started the episode having met Earl and Tate, asking about the names on her list. Earl happened to know one of the people on the list, John Lamar. They found the man in St Denis, and Hazel killed him. Hazel, Tate, and Earl went to Annsburg where she was accidentally shot by Earl who had a run in with someone. After her wounds were tended to, she set out to St. Denis where she met PC. She and PC spent time together to Thieve's Landing and the St. Denis Hotel as he heard of someone speaking about the Delacroix name. Hazel is now at the St. Denis hotel hoping to question the clerk about what they've heard.
